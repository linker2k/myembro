# Bundled sample design licenses

Checked 2026-11-01. The pack manifest lists every installed file, name, license, technique, dimensions, stitch count and SHA-256. Only `sample_*.pes` files are installed. The full CC0 text is in `CC0-1.0.txt`.

## Starter designs 2026-10 — CC0-1.0

- **AI-generated:** Images were created with OpenAI image generation (via Codex), selected by people and in some cases recolored. They were converted to stitches by myembro.
- **Dedication:** The designs are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
- **Provenance:** A per-design provenance record (prompt, date, model, edits) is kept in `docs/samples/provenance.json`.
- **Vintage tattoo flash:** Designs `F01`–`F15` and `F-sheet-130x180` are redrawn from Smithsonian American Art Museum, *Tattoo Flash* 1998.84.50A–F (CC0). Source: https://americanart.si.edu/
